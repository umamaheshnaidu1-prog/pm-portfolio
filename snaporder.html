SnapOrderAI

From messy commerce inputs to ship-ready data

View presentation ↗

01 / Problem

Small sellers receive orders as photos, PDFs and chat messages, then manually re-enter them into structured systems.

02 / Solution

Capture input, check credits, extract fields with AI, review flagged fields, and export marketplace-ready files.

03 / Product decisions

Six-week MVP roadmap; human-in-the-loop review rather than trusting every AI extraction. Capstone team: Mahesh Naidu, Smriti Garg and Vrushank.

04 / Evidence & measurement

Proposed measures: extraction accuracy, correction rate, processing time and manual-effort reduction. No measured production impact claimed.
