SaathiPay

Simplifying merchant onboarding and payments

Live prototype ↗View presentation ↗

01 / Problem

Three hypotheses: unclear documents and language, fragile video KYC, and uncertainty about payment versus settlement.

02 / Solution

A six-step onboarding journey with multilingual guidance, assisted video KYC, QR payments and settlement tracking.

03 / Product decisions

MoSCoW prioritization of four core capabilities; partner-led KYC and payments. An eight-week MVP is a planning constraint, not a completed launch.

04 / Evidence & measurement

Validate with merchant interviews and usability sessions; track onboarding completion and activation. Compliance and partner rules require validation before launch.
