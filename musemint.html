AI · product activation case study

MuseMint

Make the first creative easier

Live prototype ↗View presentation ↗

01 / Problem

A fictional cohort shows 100 signups, 60 setup completions, 35 generations and 15 saves. Prioritized blank-prompt uncertainty.

02 / Solution

Structured business and offer inputs, three styles, live image and caption generation, editing, export and regeneration.

03 / Product decisions

Save persistence and publishing are not implemented. One participant tested an earphones business and gave positive qualitative feedback.

04 / Evidence & measurement

Proposed activation: users with at least one saved post divided by all new signups. 15% to 25% is a hypothesis; track failures, time to save and AI cost.
